First touch through the queue

POST /run, then poll /status. Submitting a job is what wakes a scaled-to-zero endpoint, and the sync OpenAI route times out at ~100 s on cold calls.

Then the OpenAI route

models.list shows the served name, chat matches the model card word for word, streaming arrives in chunks.

Multimodal, embedded as base64

Images as data URIs, audio as input_audio, clips ≤ 30 s. Remote URLs fail inside the worker. The test clip was English; the prompt asked for Luganda, so the model gave Luganda.
