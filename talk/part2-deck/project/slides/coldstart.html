Levers, by impact

Model cache. Weights on local disk: 2 s instead of a multi-minute download every start.

FlashBoot. On by default; repeat starts on a recent host restore faster.

Smaller or quantized model. Load time is linear in bytes.

ENFORCE_EAGER. Skip CUDA-graph capture; flip off once throughput matters more.

Idle timeout and active workers. Warm time at $0.00031/s versus a cold start; min 1 removes cold starts for ≈ $800/month.

Client side. First call through /run, or retry on timeout; warm-up ping before a demo.

The key insight: the expensive part of a first-ever start is RunPod placing the model on hosts, and it is free. Once the hosts hold the snapshot, loading 10 GB takes two seconds; the billed two minutes are vLLM starting up. So the levers are about the engine, not the download.
