Why serverless GPUs

Pay for seconds of GPU, not for a GPU

A rented GPU

≈ $800

per month for one RTX 4090 running 24/7, whether anyone calls it or not. Most African-language traffic is bursty: quiet nights, busy mornings.

A serverless endpoint

≈ $0.04

per cold start of this model, then $0.0006 per translation request. Workers appear when jobs queue and disappear after the idle timeout.

The price you pay instead

2 min 20 s

cold start: the first request after idle waits for a worker to boot and vLLM to load the model. The rest of this talk is about making that acceptable.

Serverless fits request-response inference with idle periods. Steady 24/7 traffic is cheaper on a reserved GPU; training and notebooks belong on a pod.
