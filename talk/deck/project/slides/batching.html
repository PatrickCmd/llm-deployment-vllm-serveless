Static batching

The batch runs until its longest request finishes. Short requests leave their slots idle.

Four slots, four requests. Pale cells are idle GPU time while the queue waits.

Continuous batching

Finished requests leave and waiting ones join at every decode step.

decode steps →

Same four slots. New requests fill a gap the moment it opens, and with PagedAttention their freed blocks are back in play on the next step.
